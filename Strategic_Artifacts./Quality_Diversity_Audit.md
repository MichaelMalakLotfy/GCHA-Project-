# GCHA Quality & Diversity Audit Report

## Section 1: The Failure (ISO 25010 Context)
We have identified a "Silent Bug" in the Berlin AI model. The system systematically miscalculates cardiac risks for elderly regional patients due to biased training data. Under ISO 25010, this is a severe failure in **Functional Suitability** and **Reliability**.

## Section 2: The Decision
**Action Taken:** HALT THE ROLLOUT.
**Ethical Justification:** Under the ACM/IEEE PUBLIC Principle, software engineers must act consistently with the public interest. Releasing an AI model that discriminates against a demographic poses an unacceptable threat to human life. Launching on time is secondary to patient safety.

## Section 3: The Correction (Prevention Costs)
To correct this, we are increasing our Cost of Quality (Prevention Budget) by adding:
1. **Diverse Dataset Purchase:** $50k to acquire specialized geriatric data from rural hospitals.
2. **Regional Bias Training:** $15k to train the AI developers on algorithmic fairness.
3. **Third-Party Algorithmic Audit:** $30k to hire an external auditor to verify the new model's neutrality.
