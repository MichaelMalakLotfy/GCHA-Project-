# Global Project Calendar & Non-Working Days

## 1. Site-Specific Weekends & Holidays (Next 3 Months)
- **London (HQ):** OFF on Saturdays and Sundays.
- **Berlin (AI):** OFF on Saturdays and Sundays.
- **Bangalore (Mobile):** OFF on Saturdays and Sundays.

## 2. Schedule Assumptions (Lag & Lead Log)
We have added a **1-day Lag** to every inter-site handover to account for the "Dead Zone" communication tax caused by differing weekends and time zones.
