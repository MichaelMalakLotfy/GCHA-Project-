# GCHA Business Case & Financial Evaluation

## Section 1: Economic Summary
- **Net Present Value (NPV):** $1,250,000
- **Return on Investment (ROI):** 18.5%
- **Payback Period:** 2.4 Years
*(Note: Financials indicate a strong viable return, crossing the break-even point comfortably within the strategic timeline).*

## Section 2: Lifecycle Choice
- **Cairo (Database & Security):** V-Model (Waterfall). Chosen due to strict security requirements, rigid compliance audits, and the need for heavy upfront documentation.
- **Berlin & Bangalore (AI & Mobile App):** Agile (Scrum). Chosen to allow iterative feedback, rapid prototyping, and flexibility in UI/UX adjustments based on clinical feedback.

## Section 3: Buy vs. Build Justification
**Decision:** BUILD.
**Reasoning:** The GCHA AI predictive model is our core intellectual property. Buying an off-the-shelf system would compromise our competitive advantage and limit our ability to customize the algorithms specifically for GlobalCare's unique clinical standards.
