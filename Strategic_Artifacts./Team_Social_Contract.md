# GCHA Team Social Contract

## Section 1: The "Core Hours" Rule
To bridge the time zone gaps between London, Berlin, Cairo, and Bangalore, our mandated "Golden Window" for synchronous meetings is **10:00 AM to 12:00 PM (GMT)**. All site leads are required to be online and responsive during this window.

## Section 2: The "Response Time" SLA
- **Critical Slack Messages:** Must be acknowledged within 2 hours.
- **Email Approvals/Sign-offs:** Must be processed within 24 hours.
- **Jira Task Updates:** Must be completed daily before the developer logs off.

## Section 3: The "Conflict Escalation" Path
If a cross-site technical disagreement occurs (e.g., UI vs. API specs):
1. **Peer-to-Peer:** Leads attempt to resolve it asynchronously within 24 hours.
2. **Mediation:** If unresolved, it is escalated to the London PMO Director in the next Daily Standup.
3. **Executive Override:** If the PMO cannot resolve it, the Project Sponsor makes the final binding decision based on business value.
